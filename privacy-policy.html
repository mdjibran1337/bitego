BiteGo Fleet Partner

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BiteGo Fleet Partner ("BiteGo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the BiteGo Fleet Partner mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

2. Information We Collect

Account and identity

  • Name and profile information.
  • Phone number and/or email address.
  • Authentication information, including Google Sign-In where enabled.
  • Rider and vehicle information that you provide.

Delivery and operational information

  • Orders assigned to you and delivery information.
  • Pickup/drop-off information required for deliveries.
  • Delivery status and timestamps.
  • Earnings, incentives, payouts, and related records.

Location

With your permission, the App may collect device location, including while you are online/on duty, to support delivery assignment, navigation, tracking, safety, and operational functions.

Documents

If required for rider verification, you may upload documents such as a driving licence, vehicle registration documents, or other verification documents. These are used for verification and operational purposes.

Device and technical information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostics.
  • Crash/error and performance information where available.
  • Notification/device identifiers required for push notifications.
  • Security and reliability information.

3. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage rider accounts.
  • Authenticate riders and maintain security.
  • Assign, manage, and track deliveries.
  • Provide navigation and location-based services.
  • Verify rider and vehicle documents.
  • Calculate and process earnings, incentives, and payouts.
  • Send order updates and operational notifications.
  • Provide support.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Improve app performance and features.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party technology providers to provide its services, including:

  • Google/Firebase: authentication, database, notifications, analytics, and backend services where enabled.
  • Supabase: private storage for rider verification documents and related files where enabled.
  • Maps/location providers: mapping, navigation, geolocation, and delivery services where enabled.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Document Storage and Security

Verification documents may be stored in private cloud storage. We aim to restrict access to authorized personnel and systems using appropriate access controls and authentication.

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Only upload documents required for verification.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared as reasonably necessary to operate BiteGo, including with authorized personnel, service providers, payment/payout providers, technical infrastructure providers, or authorities where required by law.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain operational and financial records, complete verification, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, or protect legitimate business interests. Retention periods may vary by information type and applicable law.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to or correction/deletion of eligible personal information, withdraw device permissions such as location access, and ask questions about processing. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate operational, security, financial, or fraud-prevention purposes.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your BiteGo Fleet Partner account and eligible associated personal information by contacting us below. We may verify your identity before processing a request. Legally required or legitimately necessary records may be retained for the required period.

10. Children's Privacy

BiteGo Fleet Partner is intended for adult riders/partners and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, services, legal requirements, or data practices. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions, account/data deletion requests, or concerns about personal information, contact the BiteGo Fleet Partner support team.
